# Revenue Dashboard

## Purpose
Track the monetization status, models, and validation progress of active projects in the portfolio.

## How It Works
The dashboard displays the financial potential and blockers for each active quest. It is updated whenever a project validates its model or hits a revenue milestone.

## Active Projects Revenue Tracker
| Project | Revenue Model | Potential ($/mo) | Status | Time-to-Revenue | Blockers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RiftCoach** | Freemium | $500/mo | Unvalidated | 6 weeks | User feedback loop not finalized |
| **UNGASIS** | Open-Core | $1,000/mo | Unvalidated | 12 weeks | Core builder community adoption needed |

## Rules
1. **Validation Checks**: Every project must have its revenue model validated using the standard validation SOP before transitioning to active building.
2. **Review Cadence**: The potential and status must be re-evaluated during the weekly project review.
3. **Dashboard Integrity**: Only active quests listed in the portfolio should be added to the dashboard.

## Inputs/Outputs
| Component | Input Source | Output Target |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Portfolio updates + Validation logs | Weekly review report |
