# Sponsorship Model Template

## What
A model where the software app is completely free to use, and revenue is earned by displaying sponsored banner spots or partner brand mentions.

## Template
- **Audience Size Needed:** Min 1,000 monthly active users.
- **Sponsor Banner Rules:** Max 1 non-intrusive card banner in the side navigation panel.
- **Pitch copy template:**
  *"Reach [Number] active developers and no-code builders in Manila by sponsoring our dashboard card space. Slots start at [Price] per month."*

---

## Example: Manila Dev Directory
A free local resource dashboard directory for local Filipino programmers.
- **Price:** $15.00/month per active banner slot.
- **Sponsor Slots:** 3 available slots.
- **Monthly Revenue:** $45.00/month.
