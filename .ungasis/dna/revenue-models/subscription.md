# Subscription Model (SaaS) Template

## What
A model where users pay a recurring monthly or annual fee to maintain access to a software platform or database service.

## Template
- **Billing Provider:** Stripe or PayPal integrations.
- **Churn Target:** Keep monthly customer cancellation rates below 5%.

### Pricing Tiers Table
| Tier Name | Target Pricing | Included Features | Target Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbyist | Free / $0.00 | Basic features, community support | Solo builders |
| Pro | [e.g. $9.99/mo] | Unlimited templates, priority support | Professional devs |
| Team | [e.g. $29.99/mo] | Shared folders, collaborative workspaces | Micro-agencies |

---

## Example: UNGASIS Pro Membership
A monthly recurring subscription for advanced builders.
- **Price:** $9.99/month.
- **Included:** Shared MCP profiles, custom automated agent rules, and weekly metrics analysis.
