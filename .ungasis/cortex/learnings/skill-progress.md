Track the builder's technical and non-technical skills, assess progress, and log practicing milestones.

The scorecard catalogs skills with current and target levels (1-5 scale) along with concrete evidence of proficiency and practice dates.

## Rules
1. **Rating Levels**: Levels range from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Master).
2. **Evidence Requirements**: Every level upgrade must cite concrete project deliverables.
3. **Registry Standards**: Follow standard pipe-delimited format for all additions.
