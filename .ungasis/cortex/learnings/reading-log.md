# Reading Log

## Purpose
Track books, articles, and courses read by the developer, along with key takeaways and ratings to support ongoing education.

## How It Works
The log serves as a simple registry of completed reading material. Takeaways are summarized in brief statements to keep lessons actionable.

## Reading Log Tracker
| Title | Type | Date Started | Date Finished | Key Takeaways (Max 3) | Rating (1-5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules
1. **Takeaway limits**: Key takeaways must not exceed 3 items per resource.
2. **Logging Timeline**: Entries must be added within 24 hours of starting or completing a resource.
3. **Rating Scale**: Rate resources from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) based on utility to projects.

## Inputs/Outputs
| Component | Input Resource | Output Takeaways |
|---|---|---|
| Reading Log | Newly read book/article/course | Takeaway table row |
