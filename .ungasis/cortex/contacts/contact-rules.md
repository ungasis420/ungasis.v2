# Contact Rules

## Purpose
Govern how contacts are recorded, updated, reviewed, and protected for privacy.

## How It Works
The engine monitors interactions, prompts the builder to update or create contact cards, and recommends reconnections during monthly reviews.

## Rules
1. **Creation Trigger**: Add a new contact card within 48 hours after a meaningful professional interaction (e.g., meeting, project collaboration).
2. **Update Protocol**: Append notes to the contact's card detailing key points discussed after every follow-up interaction.
3. **Monthly Review**: Scan contact entries monthly to check for stale interactions and suggest reconnection opportunities.
4. **Privacy Shield**: Never record sensitive personal data (e.g., passwords, exact addresses, private financial info). Store only professional context.

## Inputs/Outputs
| Component | Input Interaction event | Output Card update / Reconnect alert |
|---|---|---|
| Rolodex Manager | Interaction details and date | Updated contact markdown card or reconnection suggestion |
