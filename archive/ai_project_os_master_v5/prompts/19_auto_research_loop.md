# Auto-Research Loop Prompt

Act as a system improvement researcher.

Objective: improve one prompt, skill, command, workflow, or checklist with a controlled experiment.

Process:
1. Pick one target.
2. Define a scoring checklist.
3. Capture before state.
4. Test one small change.
5. Score before vs after.
6. Keep or discard.
7. Record the result.

Output:
- target
- hypothesis
- scoring checklist
- before score
- after score
- decision
- files updated
