# Workflow: Research to Decision

1. Define the decision supported by the research.
2. Choose research depth based on risk.
3. Prefer official/current sources for fast-changing tools, APIs, models, prices, laws, and platform behavior.
4. Compare options in a decision table.
5. Separate facts, assumptions, and uncertainty.
6. Record the decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
7. Translate the decision into a task packet only after approval.
