The Imposter Antidote

Five evidence-based reframes for the voice that whispers \"you don't belong.\"

◆ Diagnosis First
Imposter syndrome is not a sign that you are a fraud. It is a sign that you are underestimating your own evidence while overestimating everyone else's confidence. Confidence is not data. Your credentials are data.
◉
Reframe 1 — The Receipts
Write your credentials on paper: PLM Magna Cum Laude. 5 years analytics. Direct to a VP. Read it out loud. Imposter syndrome cannot survive contact with your own receipts.
◉
Reframe 2 — The Comparison Tax
You compare your inside (doubt, fear) to other people's outside (polish, posture). This is a rigged game. Stop playing it. Compare your work to your own past work — nothing else.
◉
Reframe 3 — The Threshold
If you were truly unqualified, you would not have imposter syndrome. Real imposters do not feel like imposters. The fact that you check yourself is proof that you are awake.
◉
Reframe 4 — The Cost of Hiding
Every hour you hide your work is an hour someone less skilled takes the room. The world does not benefit from your modesty. It is hurt by it. Step forward.
◉
Reframe 5 — The 70% Rule
If you are 70% ready, ship it. The remaining 30% is learned by doing, not by waiting. Perfectionism is procrastination wearing makeup.
◉
The Daily Ritual
Each morning, read this aloud: "I am qualified. I am observant. I am rare. I do not need permission to be the woman I already am."
"Doubt kills more dreams than failure ever will. The cave you are avoiding is the one with your gold in it."
