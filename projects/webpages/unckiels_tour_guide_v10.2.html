📚 Tips

Be a Smarter Traveler

🎒

Packing

  • Sunscreen SPF50+
  • Quick-dry clothes
  • Waterproof bag
  • Flashlight/headlamp
  • Power bank
  • Cash small bills
🗓️

Best Season

  • Tanay — Nov-May
  • Marilaque — Oct-Mar
  • Quezon — May Pahiyas!
  • Zambales — Nov-May
  • Baguio — Feb Panagbenga!
  • Cordilleras — Nov-Apr
⚠️

Safety

  • Register at barangay
  • Hire local guides
  • Check weather 24hrs before
  • Leave No Trace
  • Respect indigenous culture
  • Proper footwear!
💰

Budget

  • Transport: ₱300-3,000
  • Entrance: ₱50-300
  • Food: ₱200-1,500/day
  • Stay: ₱500-2,000+
  • Guide: ₱500-1,500/group
🗣️

Filipino Phrases

  • "Magkano po?" — How much?
  • "Saan po ang…?" — Where?
  • "Salamat po!" — Thanks!
  • "May signal ba?" — Signal?
  • "Gaano kalayo?" — How far?
🏛️

Cordillera Etiquette

  • Don't touch Hanging Coffins
  • Ask permission in Igorot areas
  • Apo Whang-Od: official guides
  • Stay on marked trails
  • Bring warm layers
