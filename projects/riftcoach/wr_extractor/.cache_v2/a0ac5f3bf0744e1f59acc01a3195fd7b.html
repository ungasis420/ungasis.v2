Juggernaut
Fighters are durable and damage-focused melee champions that look to be in the thick of combat.

Juggernauts are melee titans who relentlessly march down the opposition and devastate those foolish enough to get within their grasp. They are the only subclass who excel at both dealing and taking significant amounts of damage, but in turn they have a tough time closing in on targets due to their low range and extremely limited mobility.

Examples
  • Dr. Mundo
  • Darius
  • Mordekaiser

Wild Rift content
Baron lane
Within a drafted team, the Baron lane position pushes into the Baron lane. While the Baron lane has less activity over the game, the avenue can be easily captured under enemy oversight. Being isolated from the team, a Baron lane champion needs to deal and/or sustain against damage on their own.

Suitable champions include Juggernauts, Divers, Battlemages, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Jungle

Within a drafted team, the Jungle position pushes into the Jungle. Having control of the Jungle ensures rapid reinforcement between the lanes, and safeguards key objectives that greatly bolsters the team's power.

Having to farm resources across the map, a Jungle champion needs to be efficient in clearing monsters and timing ambushes against the enemy lanes.

Suitable champions include Divers, Assassins, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Manaless

A champion that is manaless does not use nor benefit from  mana or  mana regeneration, and includes the following:

  • Energy users
  • Flow users
  • Fury users
  • Heat users
  • Health exclusive users
  • Resourceless users
League of Legends content
Blood Well

Blood Well is a resource that indicates the remaining duration of Aatrox's World Ender in seconds. It is represented beneath the health bar and is white. It fills when World Ender is used and depletes over the duration of the ability.

Wild Rift content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.

Wild Rift content
Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Support

Within a drafted team, the Support position is one of two champions pushing into the Dragon lane. Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

Suitable champions include Catchers, Enchanters, Vanguards Wardens.

Monster

Monsters are neutral units in League of Legends. Unlike minions, monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if aggroed (provoked) – with the exception of the Rift Scuttler, which simply runs away when attacked.

Champion

Champions are the player-controlled characters in League of Legends: Wild Rift. Each champion possesses unique abilities and attributes.

League of Legends content
Proc damage
Proc damage is damage type assigned to most on-hit effects. It is worth noting that many seeming on-hit effects are actually classified as ability damage and so will trigger ability effects. Some forms of on-hit damage, such as by Blade of the Ruined King and Spellblade, also benefit from  life steal.

Proc damage does not interact with most other effects, such as Luden's Echo or Arcane Comet. The main exception is:

  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Spell shield
Spell shield can negate incoming hostile abilities from applying to the recipient. This includes many active effects coming from champion abilities, epic monsters, champion summoned units, and items, but almost none from runes and summoner spells.

The shielding can vary per case with other included mechanisms such as on-hit effects and subsequent targeting
(e.g. Bouncing, Tethers).

Wild Rift content
Turret

A turret, also called a tower, is a fortification that defends a part of territory. It provides vision of the area and autonomously fires homing bolts at nearby enemies, establishing a defensive ground for allies.

Turrets are most notable in Classic, defending against waves of enemy Minions from their ultimate goal of destroying the nexus. Champions work to destroy enemy turrets to push their assault into the enemies' territory. Turrets visually fall apart as they take damage.

Wild Rift content
Ward

Wards are deployable units that that grant sight over the surrounding area. They can only be destroyed by basic attacks, granting gold and  experience.
There are three kinds of deployable wards available for purchase: Control Ward and Warding Totem.

Wild Rift content
Edge range

Edge range is calculated from the edge of a source unit's hitbox to the edge of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Edge range is mostly used for basic attacks; skill shots; and point-blank areas of effect (including auras).
  • These become more effective against large champions, as the source gains more reach against them. This may be favorable or unfavorable: durable champions tend to be larger and thus easier to hit, and frail champions tend to be smaller and more difficult to hit.
Wild Rift content
Airborne

The target is forcefully displaced, either upward (knockup) or across a distance (knockback), during which they lose control of their movement, attacks, abilities, and items.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups and channel abilities. The forced displacement interrupts dashes and overrides previous airborne effects.
League of Legends content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

League of Legends content
Centered range

Centered range is calculated from the center of a source unit's hitbox to the center of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Centered range is used for most unit-targeted abilities.
  • It is less effective and may seem to have diminished reach against large units.
Wild Rift content
Spellblade
Spellblade is a unique named item effect that grants additional effects to the next on-hit trigger after casting a champion ability.

The following items grant Spellblade:

  • Sheen
  • Iceborn Gauntlet
  • Lich Bane
  • Trinity Force

Wild Rift content
Stacks multiplicatively
An effect that stacks multiplicatively can be calculated by multiplying the effects together.

Most champion statistics that stack this way are expressed through percentages. The following stats stack multiplicatively:

  • Attack speed reduction
  • Damage modifiers
  •  Percent armor penetration and reduction
  •  Percent magic penetration and reduction
  • Slow potency reduction

Wild Rift content
Slow
The target has reduced  move speed.

Slows stack at a diminished rate. The highest amount from one source will apply in full, during which all subsequent slows will reduce to 30% effectiveness. Slow duration will refresh from the same effect.

Wild Rift content
True sight

True Sight reveals units through Fog of War, brush, and stealth. It does not bypass nearsight effects.

Wild Rift content
Tether

A tether is an effect that applies a bind buff on both the source and the target(s), which then creates the actual tether that requires both to remain in close proximity so any accompanying effects (damage, crowd control, heals, buffs, etc) can apply.

The tether breaks if it exceeds maximum range, or either conduit is removed (e.g. Death). The tether will also break from a transition into the zombie state due to the target already dying.

Generally, a bind with an enemy will break along with the tether, whereas a bind with an ally will last indefinitely and automatically reapply the tether when within range.

League of Legends content
Spell damage

Spell damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that affect targets directly, usually a single one. Spell damage applies spell effects by default.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Persistent damage
Persistent damage is the damage type dealt primarily by damage over time (DoT) abilities. Most sources of persistent damage have the magic damage sub-type, but this is not guaranteed.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage. For example:

  • Rylai's Crystal Scepter, Liandry's Torment and Luden's Echo.
  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.
  • The cooldown for Arcane Comet is reduced by 5% whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage.

Effects triggered by damage sub-types will occur (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

Wild Rift content
Basic attack reset

Some abilities and effects can reset the basic attack timer, allowing another basic attack to be initiated in quicker succession than the normal basic attack cadence.

Wild Rift content
Fear

The target is slowed and ordered to move away from the Source (the unit that casted the Fear), during which they lose control of their movement, attacks, abilities, and items. Fear will end early if the Source dies.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups and channel abilities. Overrides previous Forced Actions.
Wild Rift content
Takedown
Takedown refers to a kill or assist being acceptable, with the type of enemy specified in each use case.

Takedown is associated with triggering a passive effect, or timed effects shortly after scoring.

Wild Rift content
Resurrection
A unit that is protected by Resurrection will enter a revival state and dispel most debuffs upon taking fatal damage. After the state completes, the unit is revived. Resurrection effects do not count for kills or assists.

Resurrection sources have a hierarchy, and will trigger in the following order:

  1. Zilean's Chronoshift
  2. Guardian Angel
Resurrection will always take precedence over zombie state effects. Entering resurrection does not cancel cast times.
