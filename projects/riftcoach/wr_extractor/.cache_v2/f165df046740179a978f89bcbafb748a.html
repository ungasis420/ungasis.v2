Abilities

[edit | edit source]

The champion Kindred consists of two entities:

  • Lamb is the primary one answering movement commands and performing abilities.
  • Wolf is the secondary one acting as an untargetable companion commanded through abilities.
 ✎ 
Mark of the Kindred
TARGET IMMUNITY:
120 \ 25
Innate: Both Lamb and Wolf mark targets to hunt. Scoring a takedown against a hunted target collects a stack of Mark of the Kindred.

Mark of the Kindred: Lamb gains 75 – 250 (based on marks)  bonus attack range.
Innate - Lamb: Lamb is offered a selection of enemy champions to hunt after 6 seconds without champion combat. Once selected, she reveals the target for 2 seconds, applying the mark to hunt after 8 seconds. Lamb gains the ability to select a new target every 40 seconds, targets successfully hunted cannot be marked again for 2 minutes.
Innate - Wolf: Wolf marks a random large monster within the enemy team's jungle, highlighting on the mini map to both teams. Once the hunted target is slain, Wolf will wait 25 seconds before selecting a new target.
The type of monster that Wolf can mark will change based on current Mark of the Kindred stacks:
  • 0 : Rift Scuttler
  • 1 – 3: Rift Scuttler, Crimson Raptor, Gromp
  • 4 – 7: Ancient Krug, Blue Sentinel, Greater Murk Wolf, or Red Brambleback
  • 8 + : Rift Herald or Baron Nashor, Dragon or Elder Dragon
Kindred deal 30% bonus damage against these marked targets: small monsters, Blue Sentinel, Red Brambleback.
"Our mark looms over you."

Marksman

Marksmen (formerly "Carries") excel at dealing reliable sustained damage at range (usually through basic attacks) while constantly skirting the edge of danger. Although Marksmen have the ability to stay relatively safe by kiting their foes, they are very fragile and are extremely reliant on powerful item purchases to become true damage threats.

At present, Marksmen do not have any official subclasses. The following have been considered:

  • Demolisher
  • Scrapper
Examples
  • Ashe
  • Ezreal
  • Lucian
  • Kai'Sa
  • Jhin

Jungle
Within a drafted team, the Jungle position pushes into the Jungle. Having control of the Jungle ensures rapid reinforcement between the lanes, and safeguards key objectives that greatly bolsters the team's power.
Having to farm resources across the map, a Jungle champion needs to be efficient in clearing monsters and timing ambushes against the enemy lanes.

Suitable champions include Divers, Assassins, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Mana

 Mana (also known as MP) is a stat that is the resource of many champions necessary to cast most of their abilities. Mana can be restored via mana regeneration and some abilities, items, runes, and buffs.

Mana is represented in-game by the blue bar within the HUD, indicating the champion's current mana and maximum mana. Current mana is reduced by expenditure through paying an ability's mana cost.

Wild Rift content
Ranged

Ranged champions have a basic attack  range usually beyond 300 units. Many deal damage through projectiles, which are subject to mitigating effects like Yasuo's Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for ranged characters.

Wild Rift content
Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Support

Within a drafted team, the Support position is one of two champions pushing into the Dragon lane. Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

Suitable champions include Catchers, Enchanters, Vanguards Wardens.

Untargetable
Effects cannot hit nor target a unit that is untargetable. Effects that have already hit will not be invalidated, unless they require targetability.

Becoming untargetable will cause targeted inputs to be interrupted and non-champions to lose aggro. Most untargetability will also destroy incoming targeted projectiles.

Takedown is associated with triggering a passive effect, or timed effects shortly after scoring.

Monster

Monsters are neutral units in League of Legends. Unlike minions, monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if aggroed (provoked) – with the exception of the Rift Scuttler, which simply runs away when attacked.

Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

Wild Rift content
Sight
Sight is a property of units, structures, certain summoner spells, items, and abilities that represents a team possessing vision of a target area or unit. It is always centered range. A line of vision cannot extend beyond terrain or an unrevealed brush.

Sight can also refer to an effect that makes a unit visible regardless of the fog of war or brush, allowing them to be targeted. Standard sight cannot see stealthed units, but True Sight can.

  • Nearsight is a crowd control debuff that inhibits sight; allied vision is lost in addition to a reduced sight radius.
  • Obscured vision is similar to sight in a way that it can observe units, but does not render units targetable, rather it exposes their position and informs of their presence.
  • Unobstructed vision is a form of sight that allows a unit to be able to see their full vision radius, ignoring any obstructions that would otherwise restrict visibility.
  • All effects that use unit-targeting require sight of the target. Delayed unit-targeted attacks and spells can cancel if sight of the selected target is suddenly lost.

Basic attack reset

Some abilities and effects can reset the basic attack timer, allowing another basic attack to be initiated in quicker succession than the normal basic attack cadence.

League of Legends content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Ghosted

A unit that is ghosted ignores unit collision, allowing them to move through other units and other units to move through them.

Wild Rift content
Resurrection
A unit that is protected by Resurrection will enter a revival state and dispel most debuffs upon taking fatal damage. After the state completes, the unit is revived. Resurrection effects do not count for kills or assists.

Resurrection sources have a hierarchy, and will trigger in the following order:

  1. Zilean's Chronoshift
  2. Guardian Angel
Resurrection will always take precedence over zombie state effects. Entering resurrection does not cancel cast times.

League of Legends content
Spell damage

Spell damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that affect targets directly, usually a single one. Spell damage applies spell effects by default.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Death
Death occurs when a unit's  health is reduced to zero, thereby being killed. The unit can no longer act and is removed or becomes a corpse.

On player death, they can view the death recap and the shop to purchase items.

Wild Rift content
Minimum health threshold

The user cannot have their health reduced below the threshold by common means such as damage. The following grant this effect:

  • Kindred's Lamb's Respite
  • Pantheon's Grand Starfall
  • Tryndamere's Undying Rage

Executes will reduce the health of units with this state to 1 health. Other sources of raw damage will ignore this state.

Wild Rift content
Minion

Minions are autonomous units that spawn periodically from their nexus and advance along the lanes toward the enemy nexus. They basic attack any enemy unit or structure they find in their way, but can be killed for gold.
There are four kind of minions: / melee minions, / caster minions, / cannon minions and / super minions.

Wild Rift content
Health resource
Some champions use  Health as a resource to cast abilities.

While these champions can cast their abilities more freely than most, their disadvantage is that frequent ability usage will bring them closer to taking fatal damage.
Health costs ignore invulnerability as well as effects that prevent falling below a certain health value.

Wild Rift content
Zombie state
A buff that allows a player to keep controlling their champion after Death always flags the champion to be in a zombie state. It prevents enemies from gaining full kill credit or triggering most on-kill effects from the champion a second time, and some abilities (e.g. Yuumi's You and Me!) interact differently with champions in this state. Resurrection will always take precedence over zombie state effects.

The following effects trigger a champion to enter a zombie state:

  • Sion's Glory in Death

Wild Rift content
Clone
A clone or decoy is a specific type of pet that attempts to imitate a champion, copying their current vitality, stats, items, level, and most status effects upon creation. They are considered champions against many effects that do not strictly require player champions, else they are considered minions either small or large among certain cases.

The damage they deal is attributed to them alone, but they transfer last hit and kill credit. Killing a clone awards 25 and  44.
