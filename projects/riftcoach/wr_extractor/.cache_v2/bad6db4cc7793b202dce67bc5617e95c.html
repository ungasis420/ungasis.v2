Description
Passive: Gain bonuses upon reaching the following levels:
  • Level 1:  +6 ability haste.
  • Level 6:  +6 ability haste.
  • Level 11: When your next basic ability hits a target, its remaining cooldown is  reduced by 10% total cooldown.
Cooldown
8 seconds

Transcendence is a rune in Wild Rift.
