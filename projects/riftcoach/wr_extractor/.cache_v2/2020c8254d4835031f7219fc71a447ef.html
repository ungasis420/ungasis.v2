Teleport
✎
Active: After channeling for 3 seconds, remove any homestart or homeguard buff and blink to the target champion or ward. Your destination will be visibly marked.
Passive - [0–6:00 elapse]: You can also target allied turrets.
Cooldown
150 seconds
Breaks stealth
Yes
Maps

Teleport is a spell that blinks you to the target after a few seconds.

Notes

[edit | edit source]
  • Teleport will incur a 35-second cooldown when the game starts.

Patch History

[edit | edit source]
V6.2b
  • Cooldown reduced from 180 seconds to 150.
V6.2 - Added
  • Active: After channeling for 3 seconds, remove any homestart or homeguard buff and blink to the target champion or ward. Your destination will be visibly marked.
  • Passive - [0–6:00 elapse]: You can also target allied turrets.
  • Cooldown: 180 seconds.
