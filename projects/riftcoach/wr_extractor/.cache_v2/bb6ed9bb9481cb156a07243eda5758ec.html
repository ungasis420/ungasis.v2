Vanguard
Tanks excel in shrugging off incoming damage and focus on disrupting their enemies more than being significant damage threats themselves.

Vanguards are offensive tanks. Vanguards lead the charge for their team and specialize at bringing the action. Their explosive team fight initiation seeks to catch enemies out of position while allowing allies to follow-up to devastating effect.

Examples
  • Amumu
  • Leona
  • Maokai

Wild Rift content
Jungle
Within a drafted team, the Jungle position pushes into the Jungle. Having control of the Jungle ensures rapid reinforcement between the lanes, and safeguards key objectives that greatly bolsters the team's power.
Having to farm resources across the map, a Jungle champion needs to be efficient in clearing monsters and timing ambushes against the enemy lanes.

Suitable champions include Divers, Assassins, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Baron lane

Within a drafted team, the Baron lane position pushes into the Baron lane. While the Baron lane has less activity over the game, the avenue can be easily captured under enemy oversight.

Being isolated from the team, a Baron lane champion needs to deal and/or sustain against damage on their own.

Suitable champions include Juggernauts, Divers, Battlemages, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Support

Within a drafted team, the Support position is one of two champions pushing into the Dragon lane. Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

Suitable champions include Catchers, Enchanters, Vanguards Wardens.

Wild Rift content
Mana

 Mana (also known as MP) is a stat that is the resource of many champions necessary to cast most of their abilities. Mana can be restored via mana regeneration and some abilities, items, runes, and buffs.

Mana is represented in-game by the blue bar within the HUD, indicating the champion's current mana and maximum mana. Current mana is reduced by expenditure through paying an ability's mana cost.

Wild Rift content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.

Wild Rift content
Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Uncancellable windup
The windup of the basic attack cannot be interrupted by neither the user themself nor any status effect, other than death, polymorph, and sometimes untargetability and range.

If the attack does not lockout other abilities, they may be used normally, and the basic attack will hit during the ability cast.

Wild Rift content
Heal
Healing replenishes a unit's  current health, but never above their maximum health.

Outgoing heals can trigger bonus effects from Aery, Ardent Censer, and Staff of Flowing Waters.

Wild Rift content
Basic attack reset

Some abilities and effects can reset the basic attack timer, allowing another basic attack to be initiated in quicker succession than the normal basic attack cadence.

Wild Rift content
Dodge

Incoming attacks will miss, skipping the damage event entirely and preventing effects that trigger from damage.

Dodge affects certain attacks specified by the description. Basic attacks and abilities that apply on-hit effects are always dodgeable.

Dodge is the signature effect of Jax's Counter Strike. Blind overlaps Dodge, which overlaps Block.

Wild Rift content
Blind

The target will miss their basic attacks, which will fail to deal damage or apply on-hit effects, with the exception of some empowered basic attacks.

Wild Rift content
Block

Blocking an incoming attack will reduce the damage taken to 0, but does not prevent effects that trigger from damage.

Block affects certain attacks specified by the description. Basic attacks and abilities that apply on-hit effects are always blockable.

Block is the signature effect of Shen's Spirit's Refuge. Blind overlaps Dodge, which overlaps Block.

Spell damage
Spell damage is the damage type dealt by most single target abilities, as well as a select few basic attacks, items and runes. Spell damage is not exclusive to being applied by purely physical, magic or true damage, and instead can be dealt by any of the three damage sub-types.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of spell damage. For example:

  • Rylai's Crystal Scepter, Liandry's Torment and Luden's Echo.
  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.
  • Arcane Comet's current cooldown is reduced by 20% per instance of spell damage you deal to a champion.

Effects triggered by damage sub-types will occur (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most area of effect abilities, as well as a select few basic attacks, items and runes. Area damage is not exclusive to being applied by purely physical, magic or true damage, and instead can be dealt by any of the three damage sub-types.

  • Spell vamp heals with one-third effectiveness.
  • Many items effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of area damage (i.e. ability effects).
    • For example: Rylai's Crystal Scepter, Liandry's Torment and Luden's Echo.
    • Compared to single target spell damage, area damage does not trigger Muramana.
  • Arcane Comet's current cooldown is reduced by 10% whenever you deal an instance of area damage to a champion.
  • Area damage is reduced by 25% versus Counter Strike.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Yorick's Mist Walkers, and Naafiri's Dune Hounds take reduced damage from area damage.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes will occur (i.e. physical, magic or true).

Wild Rift content
Spell shield
Spell shield can negate incoming hostile abilities from applying to the recipient. This includes many active effects coming from champion abilities, epic monsters, champion summoned units, and items, but almost none from runes and summoner spells.

The shielding can vary per case with other included mechanisms such as on-hit effects and subsequent targeting
(e.g. Bouncing, Tethers).

Wild Rift content
Slow
The target has reduced  move speed.

Slows stack at a diminished rate. The highest amount from one source will apply in full, during which all subsequent slows will reduce to 30% effectiveness. Slow duration will refresh from the same effect.

Monster

Monsters are neutral units in League of Legends. Unlike minions, monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if aggroed (provoked) – with the exception of the Rift Scuttler, which simply runs away when attacked.

Airborne

The target is forcefully displaced, either upward (knockup) or across a distance (knockback), during which they lose control of their movement, attacks, abilities, and items.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups and channel abilities. The forced displacement interrupts dashes and overrides previous airborne effects.

Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

Untargetable
Effects cannot hit nor target a unit that is untargetable. Effects that have already hit will not be invalidated, unless they require targetability.

Becoming untargetable will cause targeted inputs to be interrupted and non-champions to lose aggro. Most untargetability will also destroy incoming targeted projectiles.

Interrupts: Interrupts movement channels.

Spell damage

Spell damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that affect targets directly, usually a single one. Spell damage applies spell effects by default.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Projectile

A projectile or missile is any entity not classified as a unit ( champion, minion, monster, etc.) that travels at any given velocity.

  • Moving units are not considered projectiles under any circumstance (including dashing units, airborne units, or units thrown by some abilities such as Syndra's Force of Will or Sion's Roar of the Slayer.).
  • Braum's Unbreakable, Samira's Blade Whirl, and Yasuo's Wind Wall can block, destroy and / or stop projectiles.
    • Some ranged basic attacks (including turrets) and abilities don't use projectiles and will bypass projectile-blocking effects.
  • Some abilities will destroy all currently incoming homing projectiles.
Wild Rift content
Sight
Sight is a property of units, structures, certain summoner spells, items, and abilities that represents a team possessing vision of a target area or unit. It is always centered range. A line of vision cannot extend beyond terrain or an unrevealed brush.

Sight can also refer to an effect that makes a unit visible regardless of the fog of war or brush, allowing them to be targeted. Standard sight cannot see stealthed units, but True Sight can.

  • Nearsight is a crowd control debuff that inhibits sight; allied vision is lost in addition to a reduced sight radius.
  • Obscured vision is similar to sight in a way that it can observe units, but does not render units targetable, rather it exposes their position and informs of their presence.
  • Unobstructed vision is a form of sight that allows a unit to be able to see their full vision radius, ignoring any obstructions that would otherwise restrict visibility.
  • All effects that use unit-targeting require sight of the target. Delayed unit-targeted attacks and spells can cancel if sight of the selected target is suddenly lost.

Brush

Brush is a common terrain feature that obstructs sight for any unit outside of it.

Minion

Minions are autonomous units that spawn periodically from their nexus and advance along the lanes toward the enemy nexus. They basic attack any enemy unit or structure they find in their way, but can be killed for gold.
There are four kind of minions: / melee minions, / caster minions, / cannon minions and / super minions.

Persistent damage
Persistent damage is the damage type dealt primarily by damage over time (DoT) abilities. Most sources of persistent damage have the magic damage sub-type, but this is not guaranteed.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage. For example:

  • Rylai's Crystal Scepter, Liandry's Torment and Luden's Echo.
  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.
  • The cooldown for Arcane Comet is reduced by 5% whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage.

Effects triggered by damage sub-types will occur (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Centered range

Centered range is calculated from the center of a source unit's hitbox to the center of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Centered range is used for most unit-targeted abilities.
  • It is less effective and may seem to have diminished reach against large units.
Wild Rift content
Edge range

Edge range is calculated from the edge of a source unit's hitbox to the edge of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Edge range is mostly used for basic attacks; skill shots; and point-blank areas of effect (including auras).
  • These become more effective against large champions, as the source gains more reach against them. This may be favorable or unfavorable: durable champions tend to be larger and thus easier to hit, and frail champions tend to be smaller and more difficult to hit.
