Assassin
Slayers (formerly "Assassins") are fragile but agile damage-focused melee champions that look to swiftly take down their targets.

Assassins specialize in infiltrating enemy lines with their unrivaled mobility to quickly dispatch high-priority targets. Due to their mostly melee nature, Assassins must put themselves into dangerous positions in order to execute their targets. Luckily, they often have defensive tricks up their sleeves that, if used cleverly, allow them to effectively avoid incoming damage.

Marksman
Marksmen (formerly "Carries") excel at dealing reliable sustained damage at range (usually through basic attacks) while constantly skirting the edge of danger. Although Marksmen have the ability to stay relatively safe by kiting their foes, they are very fragile and are extremely reliant on powerful item purchases to become true damage threats.

At present, Marksmen do not have any official subclasses. The following have been considered:

Mid
Within a drafted team, the Mid position pushes into the Mid lane. The Mid lane is the shortest path to victory, but is also a position that is easily won and lost throughout the game. Alternatively, the center of the map is the ideal base for rapid support to mobilize elsewhere.
A Mid champion would ideally have high damage for quick fights in the Mid lane.

Suitable champions include Burst Mages, Artillery Mages, and Assassins.

Mana

 Mana (also known as MP) is a stat that is the resource of many champions necessary to cast most of their abilities. Mana can be restored via mana regeneration and some abilities, items, runes, and buffs.

Mana is represented in-game by the blue bar within the HUD, indicating the champion's current mana and maximum mana. Current mana is reduced by expenditure through paying an ability's mana cost.

Ranged

Ranged champions have a basic attack  range usually beyond 300 units. Many deal damage through projectiles, which are subject to mitigating effects like Yasuo's Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for ranged characters.

Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

On-hit effect
On-hit effect is an umbrella term for many effects that trigger when you deal an instance of basic damage, such as a basic attack.

Most on-hit effects deal proc damage or default damage (aka splash damage), which are non-reactive (i.e. the effects themselves don't trigger other effects).

Wild Rift content
On-attack
On-attack effects refer to various instances that are triggered by the user when they finish their basic attack windup.

Unlike on-hit effects, on-attack effects do not require the user to land their basic attacks, only to finish their windup. Abilities that trigger on-attack effects will do so upon successfully hitting the target.

Wild Rift content
Critical strike
Certain effects can critically strike in specific circumstances—such as  critical rate—to deal increased damage. By default, basic attacks that critically strike deal 175% damage.

Critical strikes usually cannot occur against structures such as turrets and wards.

Shield
A shield adds hitpoints over the unit's  health that absorbs the next instances of damage in place of actual health. This can be improved by various items and effects depending on the type of shielding.

Incoming shields can trigger bonus effects from Shield Bash. Outgoing shields can trigger bonus effects from Aery, Ardent Censer, and Staff of Flowing Waters. Shield reduction effects, exclusively Serpent's Fang, reduce all incoming and outgoing shields.

Basic damage
Basic damage is the damage dealt by most basic attacks, and a select few abilities. Most sources of basic damage have the physical damage sub-type, but this is not guaranteed.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of basic damage. For example:

  • Wit's End and Blade of the Ruined King.
  •  Life steal, Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of basic damage dealt.
  • Basic damage is negated by dodge and block.

Effects triggered by damage sub-types will occur (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Proc damage
Proc damage is damage type assigned to most on-hit effects. It is worth noting that many seeming on-hit effects are actually classified as spell damage and so will trigger ability effects. Some forms of on-hit damage, such as by Blade of the Ruined King and Sheen Spellblade, also benefit from  life steal.

Proc damage does not interact with most other effects, such as Luden's Echo or Arcane Comet. The main exception is:

  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes will still occur in spite of proc damage's non-reactive nature (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Sight
Sight is a property of units, structures, certain summoner spells, items, and abilities that represents a team possessing vision of a target area or unit. It is always centered range. A line of vision cannot extend beyond terrain or an unrevealed brush.

Sight can also refer to an effect that makes a unit visible regardless of the fog of war or brush, allowing them to be targeted. Standard sight cannot see stealthed units, but True Sight can.

  • Nearsight is a crowd control debuff that inhibits sight; allied vision is lost in addition to a reduced sight radius.
  • Obscured vision is similar to sight in a way that it can observe units, but does not render units targetable, rather it exposes their position and informs of their presence.
  • Unobstructed vision is a form of sight that allows a unit to be able to see their full vision radius, ignoring any obstructions that would otherwise restrict visibility.
  • All effects that use unit-targeting require sight of the target. Delayed unit-targeted attacks and spells can cancel if sight of the selected target is suddenly lost.

Wild Rift content
Standard sight

Standard sight reveals units through Fog of War and brush, but not stealth. It does not bypass nearsight effects.

League of Legends content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Spell shield
Spell shield can negate incoming hostile abilities from applying to the recipient. This includes many active effects coming from champion abilities, epic monsters, champion summoned units, and items, but almost none from runes and summoner spells.

The shielding can vary per case with other included mechanisms such as on-hit effects and subsequent targeting

Wild Rift content
Takedown
Takedown refers to a kill or assist being acceptable, with the type of enemy specified in each use case.

Takedown is associated with triggering a passive effect, or timed effects shortly after scoring.

Wild Rift content
Death
Death occurs when a unit's  health is reduced to zero, thereby being killed. The unit can no longer act and is removed or becomes a corpse.

On player death, they can view the death recap and the shop to purchase items.

Wild Rift content
Camouflage

Camouflage is a form of stealth that renders a unit hidden from enemy vision for a long period of time.

Concealment is compromised against enemies within the camouflage detection radius, or when within line of sight of an enemy Control Ward (900-range). The user will receive indicators that concealment is at risk.

A stealthed unit's character model briefly shimmers for every instance of damage taken. True sight invariably reveals stealthed units.

Certain actions may break stealth, such as an attack, ability, or boot enchantment.

Wild Rift content
Brush

Brush is a common terrain feature that obstructs sight for any unit outside of it.

Wild Rift content
Clone
A clone or decoy is a specific type of pet that attempts to imitate a champion, copying their current vitality, stats, items, level, and most status effects upon creation. They are considered champions against many effects that do not strictly require player champions, else they are considered minions either small or large among certain cases.

The damage they deal is attributed to them alone, but they transfer last hit and kill credit. Killing a clone awards 25 and  44.

Wild Rift content
Zombie state
A buff that allows a player to keep controlling their champion after Death always flags the champion to be in a zombie state. It prevents enemies from gaining full kill credit or triggering most on-kill effects from the champion a second time, and some abilities (e.g. Yuumi's You and Me!) interact differently with champions in this state. Resurrection will always take precedence over zombie state effects.

The following effects trigger a champion to enter a zombie state:

  • Sion's Glory in Death

Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

Wild Rift content

Basic damage
Basic damage is the damage dealt by most basic attacks, and a select few abilities. Most sources of basic damage have the physical damage sub-type, but this is not guaranteed.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of basic damage. For example:

  • Wit's End, Blade of the Ruined King and Tiamat.
  • Plated Steelcaps will mitigate incoming basic damage on the user.
  •  Life steal and  omnivamp heal for a percentage of basic damage dealt.
  • Basic damage is negated by dodge and block.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Channel
A channel is a process that can be interrupted, by the caster themself and / or from other effects such as crowd control. Activating the channel interrupts any action currently performed.

charged channel: The player must keep the hotkey held down to charge and release to end. Unlike normal channels, the caster is still able to move freely and cast movement abilities, unless otherwise stated.

movement channel: A type of channel which also involves the caster moving from their position, usually in the form of dash or blink. Ground and root, which both disable movement effects, can also interrupt them.

Wild Rift content
True sight

True Sight reveals units through Fog of War, brush, and stealth. It does not bypass nearsight effects.
