Ixtali Seedjar
✎
Path
Inspiration
Slot
3
Description
Passive: When you or an ally hit a nearby plant, it drops a seed that you automatically collect, replacing your trinket for 60 seconds. Deploying the seed will grow the plant over 1 second and automatically destroy it, after which you cannot collect the same seed type for 30 seconds.
This rune will activate after 02:00 elapse.
Range
150 • 250 • 500

Ixtali Seedjar is a rune in Wild Rift.

Notes

[edit | edit source]
  • When you hit the plant, the seed is automatically collected if it is not on cooldown.
  • Seeds and deployed plants remain for 30 seconds.
  • Deployed plants do not drop seeds.

Patch History

[edit | edit source]
V7.1d
  • Target immunity reduced from 45 seconds to 30.
V7.1b
  • Updated the description for clarity.
V7.1
  • Slot changed from Inspiration Slot 3 to Sorcery Slot 3.
  • New: Passive: When you or an ally hit a nearby plant, it drops a seed that you automatically collect, replacing your trinket for 60 seconds. Deploying the seed will grow the plant over 1 second and automatically destroy it, after which you cannot collect the same seed type for 45 seconds. This rune will activate after 02:00 elapse.
  • Old: Passive: When you or an ally hit a nearby plant, it drops a seed that you can collect. Collecting the seed will grant you 40% bonus move speed decaying over 2.5 seconds, and replace your trinket for 60 seconds. Deploying the seed ( 300 range) will grow the plant over 1 second. Upon deployment, you cannot collect the same seed type for 45 seconds.
V5.3 - Added
  • When you or an ally hit a nearby plant, it drops a seed that you can collect. Collecting the seed will grant you 40% bonus move speed decaying over 2.5 seconds, and replace your trinket for 60 seconds.
  • Deploying the seed ( 300 range) will grow the plant over 1 second. Upon deployment, you cannot collect the same seed type for 45 seconds.
