Living Forge
Forge Disable Time:
4
Innate - Living Forge: Ornn can purchase items from anywhere on the map by forging them himself. Once Ornn selects an item to forge, he channels for 2 seconds, building the item and paying its gold cost upon completion. Ornn is unable to act for the first 1 second of the channel and the channel is interrupted if he takes damage from enemy champions or turrets. Living Forge is disabled for 4 seconds after starting the channel.
Innate: Ornn gains 7% / 12% / 17% / 22% (based on level)  bonus health,  bonus armor, and  bonus magic resist from all sources.
Innate - Master Craftsman: At levels 7, 10, and 13, Ornn gains a stack of Master Craftsman. Whenever Ornn crafts an item with Living Forge, a stack is consumed to grant the closest nearby allied champion 5 stacks of Living Forge, stacking up to 15 times and allowing them to purchase items from anywhere on the map once for each stack.

Vanguard
Tanks excel in shrugging off incoming damage and focus on disrupting their enemies more than being significant damage threats themselves.

Vanguards are offensive tanks. Vanguards lead the charge for their team and specialize at bringing the action. Their explosive team fight initiation seeks to catch enemies out of position while allowing allies to follow-up to devastating effect.

Examples
  • Amumu
  • Leona
  • Maokai

Wild Rift content
Baron lane
Within a drafted team, the Baron lane position pushes into the Baron lane. While the Baron lane has less activity over the game, the avenue can be easily captured under enemy oversight. Being isolated from the team, a Baron lane champion needs to deal and/or sustain against damage on their own.

Suitable champions include Juggernauts, Divers, Battlemages, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Mana

 Mana (also known as MP) is a stat that is the resource of many champions necessary to cast most of their abilities. Mana can be restored via mana regeneration and some abilities, items, runes, and buffs.

Mana is represented in-game by the blue bar within the HUD, indicating the champion's current mana and maximum mana. Current mana is reduced by expenditure through paying an ability's mana cost.

Wild Rift content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.

Wild Rift content
Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Wild Rift content
Support

Within a drafted team, the Support position is one of two champions pushing into the Dragon lane. Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

Suitable champions include Catchers, Enchanters, Vanguards Wardens.

Channel
A channel is a process that can be interrupted, by the caster themself and / or from other effects such as crowd control. Activating the channel interrupts any action currently performed.

charged channel: The player must keep the hotkey held down to charge and release to end. Unlike normal channels, the caster is still able to move freely and cast movement abilities, unless otherwise stated.

movement channel: A type of channel which also involves the caster moving from their position, usually in the form of dash or blink. Ground and root, which both disable movement effects, can also interrupt them.

Wild Rift content
lockout
A champion that is locked out is unable to perform certain actions such as casting abilities, moving, or attacking. The prevented actions can vary per instance.

Lockout does not interact with crowd control mechanics unless otherwise stated.

Wild Rift content
Interrupt

Interrupt is a quality of crowd control that interrupts an in-progress effect, preventing its completion or causing the effect to go off at an interrupted state.

The following crowd control effects interrupt channeled and charged abilities (unless otherwise noted by the ability):

  • Airborne
  • Forced action ( Charm, Flee, Taunt)
  • Silence ( Disrupt, Polymorph)
  • Sleep
  • Stasis
  • Stun ( Suspension)
  • Suppression

The following crowd control effects interrupt dashes:

  • Airborne
  • Knockdown
  • Stasis

Basic attack windups are interrupted by all forms of crowd control that inhibit attacking. Ground and Root only interrupt channeled and charged movement abilities.

Champion

Champions are the player-controlled characters in League of Legends: Wild Rift. Each champion possesses unique abilities and attributes.

Wild Rift content
Turret

A turret, also called a tower, is a fortification that defends a part of territory. It provides vision of the area and autonomously fires homing bolts at nearby enemies, establishing a defensive ground for allies.

Turrets are most notable in Classic, defending against waves of enemy Minions from their ultimate goal of destroying the nexus. Champions work to destroy enemy turrets to push their assault into the enemies' territory. Turrets visually fall apart as they take damage.

League of Legends content
Proc damage
Proc damage is damage type assigned to most on-hit effects. It is worth noting that many seeming on-hit effects are actually classified as ability damage and so will trigger ability effects. Some forms of on-hit damage, such as by Blade of the Ruined King and Spellblade, also benefit from  life steal.

Proc damage does not interact with most other effects, such as Luden's Echo or Arcane Comet. The main exception is:

  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Slow
The target has reduced  move speed.

Slows stack at a diminished rate. The highest amount from one source will apply in full, during which all subsequent slows will reduce to 30% effectiveness. Slow duration will refresh from the same effect.

Airborne

The target is forcefully displaced, either upward (knockup) or across a distance (knockback), during which they lose control of their movement, attacks, abilities, and items.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups and channel abilities. The forced displacement interrupts dashes and overrides previous airborne effects.
League of Legends content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Brittle

A unit afflicted with Brittle has their Tenacity reduced by 30%.

Brittle's duration ends prematurely if the affected unit becomes immobilized.

Tenacity (or Crowd Control Reduction) is a stat that reduces the duration of all incoming crowd control effects except displacements, drowsy, nearsight, stasis, and suppression. If tenacity is reduced below 0, the crowd control duration increases.

Immobilize
The target loses control of their movement. The following crowd control effects immobilize the target:
  • Airborne
  • Forced Action ( Charm, Fear, Taunt)
  • Root
  • Sleep
  • Stasis
  • Stun ( Suspension, Suppression)

Impaired movement is a similar term that refers to targets that are slowed in addition to the effects above.

Monster

Monsters are neutral units in League of Legends. Unlike minions, monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if aggroed (provoked) – with the exception of the Rift Scuttler, which simply runs away when attacked.

Wild Rift content
Lunge
A lunge refers to the movement of a champion during an ability's cast animation (such as short-ranged abilities) or an empowered attack's animation (such as empowerments that grant  bonus range), in order to place the champion in range for a follow-up attack.

Lunge is a movement distinct from dashes, primarily by not triggering effects that would otherwise interact with dashes, such as Steadfast Presence and Doom 'n Gloom.

There are four kind of minions: / melee minions, / caster minions, / cannon minions and / super minions.

Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

Wild Rift content
Sight
Sight is a property of units, structures, certain summoner spells, items, and abilities that represents a team possessing vision of a target area or unit. It is always centered range. A line of vision cannot extend beyond terrain or an unrevealed brush.

Sight can also refer to an effect that makes a unit visible regardless of the fog of war or brush, allowing them to be targeted. Standard sight cannot see stealthed units, but True Sight can.

  • Nearsight is a crowd control debuff that inhibits sight; allied vision is lost in addition to a reduced sight radius.
  • Obscured vision is similar to sight in a way that it can observe units, but does not render units targetable, rather it exposes their position and informs of their presence.
  • Unobstructed vision is a form of sight that allows a unit to be able to see their full vision radius, ignoring any obstructions that would otherwise restrict visibility.
  • All effects that use unit-targeting require sight of the target. Delayed unit-targeted attacks and spells can cancel if sight of the selected target is suddenly lost.

Spellblade
Spellblade is a unique named item effect that grants additional effects to the next on-hit trigger after casting a champion ability.

The following items grant Spellblade:

  • Sheen
  • Iceborn Gauntlet
  • Lich Bane
  • Trinity Force

Spell shield
Spell shield can negate incoming hostile abilities from applying to the recipient. This includes many active effects coming from champion abilities, epic monsters, champion summoned units, and items, but almost none from runes and summoner spells.

The shielding can vary per case with other included mechanisms such as on-hit effects and subsequent targeting
(e.g. Bouncing, Tethers).
