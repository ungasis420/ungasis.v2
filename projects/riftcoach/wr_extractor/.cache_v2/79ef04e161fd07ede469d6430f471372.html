The Darkin Scythe
Innate: Kayn has a secondary experience bar that progresses toward unlocking his Darkin and Shadow forms. He gains experience toward either form by taking down or dealing damage against melee or ranged champions (for Darkin and Shadow, respectively). Each completed form will appear at the summoning platform. Kayn can collect it to permanently transform over 6 seconds, restoring to full health and mana and becoming invulnerable, untargetable, and unable to act. The transformation will grant a passive bonus and empower some of Kayn's abilities. He gains a one-time Homeguard once the transformation is complete.
Shadow Bonus: For 3 seconds upon entering champion combat, Kayn deals 48% – 62% (based on level) of post-mitigation damage dealt against champions as bonus magic damage. This effect is primed after 8 seconds without champion combat, or upon casting Umbral Trespass.
Darkin Bonus: Rhaast's abilities will heal for 24% – 38% (based on level) of post-mitigation dealt against champions.
Transformation alert.

Experience System

The experience is depicted as orbs that drop upon the ground. Melee champions drop red orbs that grant progress for Darkin; ranged champions drop blue orbs that grant progress for Shadow. Orbs will linger until automatically collected by Kayn regardless of range once out of combat or dead.

Skirmisher
Slayers (formerly "Assassins") are fragile but agile damage-focused melee champions that look to swiftly take down their targets.

Skirmishers (also known as Duelists) aim to shred through any nearby enemy that approaches. Because Skirmishers lack high-end burst damage or reliable ways of closing in on high-priority targets, they are instead armed with situationally powerful defensive tools to survive in the fray, along with extreme sustained damage to cut down even the most durable targets.

Examples
  • Fiora
  • Jax
  • Yasuo

Jungle
Within a drafted team, the Jungle position pushes into the Jungle. Having control of the Jungle ensures rapid reinforcement between the lanes, and safeguards key objectives that greatly bolsters the team's power.
Having to farm resources across the map, a Jungle champion needs to be efficient in clearing monsters and timing ambushes against the enemy lanes.

Suitable champions include Divers, Assassins, Skirmishers, and Vanguards.

Wild Rift content
Mana

 Mana (also known as MP) is a stat that is the resource of many champions necessary to cast most of their abilities. Mana can be restored via mana regeneration and some abilities, items, runes, and buffs.

Mana is represented in-game by the blue bar within the HUD, indicating the champion's current mana and maximum mana. Current mana is reduced by expenditure through paying an ability's mana cost.

Wild Rift content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.

Wild Rift content
Attack speed ratio

The Attack speed ratio (AS ratio) adjusts the effectiveness of bonus AS from all sources. It is usually equal to the unit's base AS (level 1 with no bonuses), but tuning to a higher or lower relative value translates to more or less bonus AS received compared to other units with the same base AS.

Total AS = Base AS + Bonus AS × AS Ratio
(All values in decimal form)

The ingame stats HUD displays the AS ratio instead of base AS to communicate this comparative effectiveness. The Wiki opts to always list both stats and show the AS ratio as "N/A" if the two are equal.

Support

Within a drafted team, the Support position is one of two champions pushing into the Dragon lane. Securing the Dragon lane will allow access to the Dragon, an objective that can influence the rest of the game. However, the Support may not be able to grow at the same rate of other positions due to a lack of income, and will instead prefer efficiency to their power.

A Support champion will work best by either supplying utility or setting up opportunities to kill the enemy.

Suitable champions include Catchers, Enchanters, Vanguards Wardens.

Wild Rift content
Takedown
Takedown refers to a kill or assist being acceptable, with the type of enemy specified in each use case.

Takedown is associated with triggering a passive effect, or timed effects shortly after scoring.

Wild Rift content
Ranged

Ranged champions have a basic attack  range usually beyond 300 units. Many deal damage through projectiles, which are subject to mitigating effects like Yasuo's Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for ranged characters.

Wild Rift content
Invulnerability

A unit that is Invulnerable reduces all damage taken to 0 and prevents effects that trigger from damage.

Wild Rift content
Untargetable
Effects cannot hit nor target a unit that is untargetable. Effects that have already hit will not be invalidated, unless they require targetability.

Becoming untargetable will cause targeted inputs to be interrupted and non-champions to lose aggro. Most untargetability will also destroy incoming targeted projectiles.

Wild Rift content
lockout
A champion that is locked out is unable to perform certain actions such as casting abilities, moving, or attacking. The prevented actions can vary per instance.

Lockout does not interact with crowd control mechanics unless otherwise stated.

Wild Rift content
Heal
Healing replenishes a unit's  current health, but never above their maximum health.

Outgoing heals can trigger bonus effects from Aery, Ardent Censer, and Staff of Flowing Waters.

Wild Rift content
Homing projectile destruction
Destroying or popping of a projectile references an effect that destroys all homing missiles currently targeting a specific unit, such as ranged projectile-based basic attacks. Destroyed missiles will do the same thing as if they were destroyed by Yasuo's Wind Wall or Samira's Blade Whirl (usually vanishing without any effect).

The following abilities perform one or more projectile popping events:

  • Most abilities and effects that grant untargetability pop projectiles when the unit gains the status effect.
    • Master Yi's Alpha Strike in particular pops targeted projectiles on each game tick for its duration.
    • Gwen's Hallowed Mist pops all projectiles (from enemy champions outside of its area of effect) targeted at her that attempt to pass through or be inside its area of effect.
  • Upon entering or leaving Mordekaiser's Realm of Death.
  • Upon successfully completing the channel of:
    • Shen's Stand United
    • Twisted Fate's Gate
    • Teleport Enchant

Wild Rift content
Projectile

A projectile or missile is any entity not classified as a unit ( champion, minion, monster, etc.) that travels at any given velocity.

  • Moving units are not considered projectiles under any circumstance (including dashing units, airborne units, or units thrown by some abilities such as Syndra's Force of Will or Sion's Roar of the Slayer.).
  • Braum's Unbreakable, Samira's Blade Whirl, and Yasuo's Wind Wall can block, destroy and / or stop projectiles.
    • Some ranged basic attacks (including turrets) and abilities don't use projectiles and will bypass projectile-blocking effects.
  • Some abilities will destroy all currently incoming homing projectiles.

Death
Death occurs when a unit's  health is reduced to zero, thereby being killed. The unit can no longer act and is removed or becomes a corpse.

On player death, they can view the death recap and the shop to purchase items.

Dash
You move to a specific direction or location while traversing the intervening distance. Dashes can cross terrain unless otherwise stated.

  • Dashes will not cross terrain if you would end within terrain, some dashes gain increased distance when directed through terrain.
  • Dashes are overridden by all displacements, including airborne and other dashes.
  • Dashes are interrupted by stasis, knockdown, and blink.

There are four kind of minions: / melee minions, / caster minions, / cannon minions and / super minions.

Monster

Monsters are neutral units in League of Legends. Unlike minions, monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if aggroed (provoked) – with the exception of the Rift Scuttler, which simply runs away when attacked.

League of Legends content
Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Spell shield
Spell shield can negate incoming hostile abilities from applying to the recipient. This includes many active effects coming from champion abilities, epic monsters, champion summoned units, and items, but almost none from runes and summoner spells.

The shielding can vary per case with other included mechanisms such as on-hit effects and subsequent targeting
(e.g. Bouncing, Tethers).

Wild Rift content
Interrupt

Interrupt is a quality of crowd control that interrupts an in-progress effect, preventing its completion or causing the effect to go off at an interrupted state.

The following crowd control effects interrupt channeled and charged abilities (unless otherwise noted by the ability):

  • Airborne
  • Forced action ( Charm, Flee, Taunt)
  • Silence ( Disrupt, Polymorph)
  • Sleep
  • Stasis
  • Stun ( Suspension)
  • Suppression

The following crowd control effects interrupt dashes:

  • Airborne
  • Knockdown
  • Stasis

Basic attack windups are interrupted by all forms of crowd control that inhibit attacking. Ground and Root only interrupt channeled and charged movement abilities.

Wild Rift content
Slow
The target has reduced  move speed.

Slows stack at a diminished rate. The highest amount from one source will apply in full, during which all subsequent slows will reduce to 30% effectiveness. Slow duration will refresh from the same effect.

Wild Rift content
Airborne

The target is forcefully displaced, either upward (knockup) or across a distance (knockback), during which they lose control of their movement, attacks, abilities, and items.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups and channel abilities. The forced displacement interrupts dashes and overrides previous airborne effects.
Wild Rift content
Heal
Healing replenishes a unit's  current health, but never above their maximum health.

Outgoing heals can trigger bonus effects from Aery, Ardent Censer, and Staff of Flowing Waters.

Wild Rift content
Unobstructed vision
A unit with unobstructed sight can see the full circle of their vision radius, ignoring all obstructions and being able to see into terrain. This effect does not allow the user to see into brush, although they will be able to see beyond the brush if within range.

Having unobstructed sight will benefit all forms of vision, including true sight and camouflage detection - i.e. you will be able to reveal stealthed units over walls where usually terrain would block true sight. Nearsight prevents this vision entirely; the user's sight radius will not infiltrate beyond terrain.

Wild Rift content
Immobilize
The target loses control of their movement. The following crowd control effects immobilize the target:
  • Airborne
  • Forced Action ( Charm, Fear, Taunt)
  • Root
  • Sleep
  • Stasis
  • Stun ( Suspension, Suppression)

Impaired movement is a similar term that refers to targets that are slowed in addition to the effects above.

Polymorph

The target is silenced and turned into a helpless critter.

Silence: The target loses control of their abilities and items.

Polymorph is the signature effect of Lulu's Whimsy, which also disarms the target and reduces their move speed.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups (including uncancellable) and channel abilities.
Wild Rift content
Slow resist

Slow resist is a stat that directly reduces the effectiveness of slows.

Wild Rift content
Stacks multiplicatively
An effect that stacks multiplicatively can be calculated by multiplying the effects together.

Most champion statistics that stack this way are expressed through percentages. The following stats stack multiplicatively:

  • Attack speed reduction
  • Damage modifiers
  •  Percent armor penetration and reduction
  •  Percent magic penetration and reduction
  • Slow potency reduction

Wild Rift content
Camouflage

Camouflage is a form of stealth that renders a unit hidden from enemy vision for a long period of time.

Concealment is compromised against enemies within the camouflage detection radius, or when within line of sight of an enemy Control Ward (900-range). The user will receive indicators that concealment is at risk.

A stealthed unit's character model briefly shimmers for every instance of damage taken. True sight invariably reveals stealthed units.

Certain actions may break stealth, such as an attack, ability, or boot enchantment.

Wild Rift content
Vanish
In addition to granting untargetability, vanish briefly hides a unit from the game's renderer, which causes it to become completely undetectable and invisible even to effects such as Sweeping Lens. Vanished units are unable to act. As with all untargetability, tethers will still persist on a target that vanishes.

Vanish is not considered stealth and does not interact with sight effects.

Wild Rift content
Channel
A channel is a process that can be interrupted, by the caster themself and / or from other effects such as crowd control. Activating the channel interrupts any action currently performed.

charged channel: The player must keep the hotkey held down to charge and release to end. Unlike normal channels, the caster is still able to move freely and cast movement abilities, unless otherwise stated.

movement channel: A type of channel which also involves the caster moving from their position, usually in the form of dash or blink. Ground and root, which both disable movement effects, can also interrupt them.

League of Legends content
Attached
Attached refers to the state of a unit whose movement is bound entirely to another object, such as a unit or missile. They will be at the current location of the unit they are attached to at all times, or at a specific offset to that location.

A unit can either attach themselves to another unit or attach another unit to themselves. In any case, the unit that has units attached to them can move those units by any means of movement, whether it be by pathing (movement commands), displacement ( dashes and forced displacement), or blinks. The unit that is being attached to another unit however cannot move from their current location by themselves by any means, including the use of the aforementioned movement methods.

Wild Rift content
True sight

True Sight reveals units through Fog of War, brush, and stealth. It does not bypass nearsight effects.

League of Legends content
Spell damage

Spell damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that affect targets directly, usually a single one. Spell damage applies spell effects by default.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Wild Rift content
Spellblade
Spellblade is a unique named item effect that grants additional effects to the next on-hit trigger after casting a champion ability.

The following items grant Spellblade:

  • Sheen
  • Iceborn Gauntlet
  • Lich Bane
  • Trinity Force

Wild Rift content
Resurrection
A unit that is protected by Resurrection will enter a revival state and dispel most debuffs upon taking fatal damage. After the state completes, the unit is revived. Resurrection effects do not count for kills or assists.

Resurrection sources have a hierarchy, and will trigger in the following order:

  1. Zilean's Chronoshift
  2. Guardian Angel
Resurrection will always take precedence over zombie state effects. Entering resurrection does not cancel cast times.

Wild Rift content
Clone
A clone or decoy is a specific type of pet that attempts to imitate a champion, copying their current vitality, stats, items, level, and most status effects upon creation. They are considered champions against many effects that do not strictly require player champions, else they are considered minions either small or large among certain cases.

The damage they deal is attributed to them alone, but they transfer last hit and kill credit. Killing a clone awards 25 and  44.

Wild Rift content
Turret

A turret, also called a tower, is a fortification that defends a part of territory. It provides vision of the area and autonomously fires homing bolts at nearby enemies, establishing a defensive ground for allies.

Turrets are most notable in Classic, defending against waves of enemy Minions from their ultimate goal of destroying the nexus. Champions work to destroy enemy turrets to push their assault into the enemies' territory. Turrets visually fall apart as they take damage.
