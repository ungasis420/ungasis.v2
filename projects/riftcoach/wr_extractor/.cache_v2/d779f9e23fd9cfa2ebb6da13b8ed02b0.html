Description
Passive: Gain 3% (+ 1% per Legend stack) tenacity and 3% (+ 1.416% per Legend stack) slow resist, up to 15% tenacity and 20% slow resist at maximum stacks.
Legend: Gain Legend stacks for every 15 – 30 (based on stacks) points earned, up to 12:
  • 15 points for champion takedowns
  • 15 points for epic monster takedowns.
  • 5 points for large monster kills.
  • 3 points for minion kills.

Legend: Tenacity is a rune in Wild Rift.

Tenacity

Tenacity (or Crowd Control Reduction) is a stat that reduces the duration of all incoming crowd control effects except displacements, drowsy, nearsight, stasis, and suppression.

If tenacity is reduced below 0, the crowd control duration increases.

  • Tenacity stacks multiplicatively or additively based on the combination of effects.
  • The duration of a crowd control debuff is calculated when it is applied, and tenacity can reduce it to a minimum 0.5 seconds.

League of Legends: Wild Rift

League of Legends: Wild Rift (also called Wild Rift or WR) is a multiplayer online battle arena game currently available for iOS and Android devices. The game is developed by Riot Games, a subsidiary of Tencent. It is an abridged version of its PC counterpart, League of Legends, with its own content releases and a shared (though not identical) champion roster.
