Smite
✎
Deal 600 true damage to the target large or epic monster or minion and  heal for 70 + (10% maximum health).
Transforms into Chilling Smite after 3 uses.
Passive - Jungle Expertise:
  • Earn 20% bonus experience and gold from monsters; bonus gold is disabled after 11:00 elapse. Earn 60% reduced experience and gold from minions until 06:00 elapse.
  • Against monsters, your attacks deal 15% bonus physical damage, and your abilities deal 30% bonus physical damage.
  • Attacking monsters restores 40 health over 5 seconds.
  • Gain 20 mana regen while in jungle or river.
Passive - Conservation: Generate 6 stacks every 5 seconds, stacking up to 180 stacks. When you kill a large or epic monster, consume all stacks to gain 1 per stack. Generation increases to 12 stacks per 5 seconds if your team's total gold is lower than the enemy by at least 10%, and your total gold is lower than your allies' average total gold by at least 5%.

Chilling Smite
✎
Deal 1000 true damage to the target large or epic monster or minion and  heal for 70 + (10% maximum health).
Chilling Smite can be cast on enemy champions, dealing 38 – 184 (based on level) true damage and slowing the target by 25% for 2 seconds, while also granting you 25% bonus movement speed for the same duration.
Passive - Jungle Expertise:
  • Earn 20% bonus experience and gold from monsters; bonus gold is disabled after 11:00 elapse. Earn 60% reduced experience and gold from minions until 06:00 elapse.
  • Against monsters, your attacks deal 15% bonus physical damage, and your abilities deal 30% bonus physical damage.
  • Attacking monsters restores 40 health over 5 seconds.
  • Gain 20 mana regen while in jungle or river.
Passive - Conservation: Generate 6 stacks every 5 seconds, up to 180 stacks. When you kill a large or epic monster, consume all stacks to gain 1 per stack. Generation increases to 12 stacks per 5 seconds if your team's total gold is lower than the enemy by at least 10%, and your total gold is lower than your allies' average total gold by at least 5%.

Champion

Champions are the player-controlled characters in League of Legends: Wild Rift. Each champion possesses unique abilities and attributes.

Wild Rift content
Slow
The target has reduced  move speed.

Slows stack at a diminished rate. The highest amount from one source will apply in full, during which all subsequent slows will reduce to 30% effectiveness. Slow duration will refresh from the same effect.

Wild Rift content
Edge range

Edge range is calculated from the edge of a source unit's hitbox to the edge of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Edge range is mostly used for basic attacks; skill shots; and point-blank areas of effect (including auras).
  • These become more effective against large champions, as the source gains more reach against them. This may be favorable or unfavorable: durable champions tend to be larger and thus easier to hit, and frail champions tend to be smaller and more difficult to hit.
Wild Rift content
Persistent damage
Persistent damage is the damage type dealt primarily by damage over time (DoT) abilities. Most sources of persistent damage have the magic damage sub-type, but this is not guaranteed.

Many effects are triggered whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage. For example:

  • Rylai's Crystal Scepter, Liandry's Torment and Luden's Echo.
  • Omnivamp and Physical vamp (where applicable) heal for a percentage of damage dealt.
  • The cooldown for Arcane Comet is reduced by 5% whenever you deal an instance of persistent damage.

Effects triggered by damage sub-types will occur (e.g. Black Cleaver on instances of Physical damage and Morellonomicon on instances of Magic damage).
