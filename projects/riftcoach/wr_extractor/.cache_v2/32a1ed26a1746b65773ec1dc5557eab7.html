Description
Passive: Damaging attacks and abilities against an enemy champion below 50% health will deal 40 (+ 10 per stack) (+ 25% bonus AD) (+ 15% AP) bonus adaptive damage, then generate 1 stack.
The cooldown  reduces to 1 second upon scoring a champion takedown.
Adaptive Damage: This effect deals either physical or magic damage depending on whether you have more  bonus attack damage or  ability power.
  • Greater  bonus attack damage → Physical damage
  • Greater  ability power → Magic damage
If the damage contribution of  AD and  AP are zero or otherwise equal, the damage type depends on the champion's adaptive type.
Cooldown
35 seconds

Adaptive damage

Adaptive Damage: This effect deals either physical or magic damage depending on the damage contribution from your  attack damage and  ability power to the effect's damage formula.

  • Greater bonus damage from the  AD ratio → Physical damage
  • Greater bonus damage from the  AP ratio → Magic damage

If the damage contribution of  AD and  AP are zero or otherwise equal, the damage type depends on the effect's particular default.

Takedown
Takedown refers to a kill or assist being acceptable, with the type of enemy specified in each use case.

Takedown is associated with triggering a passive effect, or timed effects shortly after scoring.

League of Legends: Wild Rift

League of Legends: Wild Rift (also called Wild Rift or WR) is a multiplayer online battle arena game currently available for iOS and Android devices. The game is developed by Riot Games, a subsidiary of Tencent. It is an abridged version of its PC counterpart, League of Legends, with its own content releases and a shared (though not identical) champion roster.
