# Local Repo Drop-In Guide — Chunk 1

1. Unzip this package.
2. Copy `public/data/*.json` into your repo's `public/data/` folder.
3. Keep `database/`, `schemas/`, `source_policy/`, `quality/`, `architecture/`, and `ai/` at repo root or under `/data-platform/`.
4. Do not replace your existing champion/item/rune/spell runtime data yet. Chunk 1 is rules and architecture.
5. Use these files as validators before importing later chunks.
