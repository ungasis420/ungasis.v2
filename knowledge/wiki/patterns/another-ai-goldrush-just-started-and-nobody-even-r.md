# Another AI Goldrush Just Started (and nobody even realises)

> Source: YouTube
> URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ioJ0dCbeWTs

## Summary
- **Who:** JP Middleton — AI agency owner ($25M+ revenue), 16.8K subscribers, founder of StartGrowSell.ai
- **What:** A blueprint for building a 7-figure AI agency by servicing boring local brick-and-mortar businesses (gyms, dentists, med spas, HVAC, roofers) — not tech companies
- **When:** ~June 10, 2026 (3 days ago per screenshot, 13K views)
- **Where:** YouTube (18:23 video) — targeting aspiring AI agency owners, freelancers, and solopreneurs
- **Why:** Because there's a gap inside the gap — everyone's chasing flashy AI agents and vibe-coded apps for the internet economy, while old-economy business owners are desperate for AI help but can't relate to tech nerds. That untapped gap is the real gold rush.
- **How:** By deploying what he calls the "Aristotle System" — 6 AI employees installed as one end-to-end system for local businesses, plus Facebook ads for lead generation and a proven sales closing process

## Key Takeaways
- This is essentially the "sell the shovels" play — but applied to AI agencies servicing the old economy. The Aristotle System's sequential logic (fix retention → social proof → lead capture → phone coverage → sales skills → THEN ads) is actually a solid architecture pattern. Think of it like your UNGASIS cognitive architecture — each "AI employee" is like an engine, and they only work when wired together as a system. 🍳 Kitchen analogy: it's like setting up the entire kitchen (prep station, grill, fryer, plating, expo) before opening the restaurant — not just buying one fancy oven and hoping for the best.
